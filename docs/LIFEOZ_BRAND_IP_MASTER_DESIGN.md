# LIFEOZ — Brand / IP Master Design

**Status:** LOCKED PRODUCT-DESIGN SPECIFICATION v1.0  
**Purpose:** Single source of truth for the LIFEOZ visual identity, interaction language, Yansi identity, core symbols, and protection-oriented documentation before further APK implementation.

> This document is a product/design specification, not a legal registration or a statement that trademark, copyright, design, or patent rights have already been granted.

## 1. Product Identity

**Product:** LIFEOZ  
**Positioning:** A living personal operating system for life.  
**Core promise:** One screen. One tap. One intelligence.  
**AI identity:** Yansi — ambient personal intelligence.  
**Design principle:** Less information on screen; more intelligence behind the screen.

LIFEOZ must feel like an intelligent environment rather than a conventional productivity dashboard. Conventional dashboard cards, generic neon decoration, generic Material iconography, and chatbot-first layouts are not part of the final identity.

## 2. LIFEOZ Brand Mark

The final LIFEOZ mark must be a custom geometric symbol created specifically for LIFEOZ. The current concept-art infinity/8-like mark is **not automatically the final legal mark** and must not be represented as legally registered.

Brand symbolism:
- continuity of life
- intelligence connecting life domains
- evolution over time
- balance between human agency and AI
- a central point of intelligence with connected life states

The final master logo set must contain:
1. Primary symbol + LIFEOZ wordmark
2. Symbol-only app icon
3. Monochrome mark
4. Small-size mark
5. Splash/boot mark
6. Safe-area specifications

No generated artwork may be labelled “registered”, “patented”, or “trademarked” until the appropriate legal status actually exists.

## 3. Yansi Identity

Yansi is not the LIFEOZ logo and is not a conventional chatbot avatar.

Yansi is represented by a living, adaptive intelligence field. Its geometry changes with state:
- listening: inward resonance / synchronized motion
- understanding: structures converge
- reasoning: connected layers reorganize
- acting: controlled outward energy
- quiet: low-energy ambient presence
- warning: restrained high-salience state

Yansi must remain quiet unless there is a useful reason to interrupt. It must never fabricate actions, silently perform sensitive actions, or rewrite its own application behaviour.

## 4. Five Life Domains — Custom Glyph System

The home screen uses symbols rather than permanent written core names.

### A. Life Genesis
Meaning: goals, growth, habits, milestones, life direction.
Glyph language: an evolving seed-to-constellation form, not a stock tree icon.

### B. Vital Guardian
Meaning: health, wellness, family and personal care.
Glyph language: a protective pulse geometry, not a stock heart icon.

### C. Wealth Flow
Meaning: expenses, income, savings and investments.
Glyph language: a circulating resource-flow glyph, not a stock wallet/coins icon.

### D. Time Harmony
Meaning: tasks, calendar, bills, renewals, reminders and commitments.
Glyph language: a temporal orbit / phase marker, not a stock calendar or clock icon.

### E. Knowledge Core
Meaning: diary, notes, ideas, learning and documents.
Glyph language: a layered cognition/knowledge lattice, not a stock brain icon.

Each glyph must have a unique silhouette, animation, active-state behaviour and sound cue. The same five glyphs are not simply recoloured between realities; each visual reality has a corresponding visual treatment while preserving semantic recognition.

## 5. Six Visual Realities

These are six different visual systems, not six colour themes.

1. **OREON PRIME** — living cosmic organism; flowing intelligent filaments and energy topology.
2. **TERRA FLUX** — organic adaptive environment; bio-luminescent growth and natural intelligence.
3. **VORTEX NEXUS** — dimensional intelligence; layered orbital depth and controlled spatial distortion.
4. **CRYSTA LUMEN** — precision light geometry; faceted intelligence structures and refractive transitions.
5. **NEBULA SOUL** — emotional/energy environment; expressive atmospheric fields and state-sensitive motion.
6. **SHADOW CORE** — minimal dark-matter intelligence; sparse, precise, high-contrast spatial interaction.

A selected reality changes the entire visual grammar: background topology, glyph construction, Yansi geometry, transitions, depth, motion, typography treatment and information presentation.

## 6. Home Environment

The home screen must not resemble a conventional dashboard.

Required behaviour:
- central Yansi presence
- five semantic glyphs arranged as an adaptive spatial system
- no permanent core names
- subtle living background topology
- information appears only when relevant
- important insights alter the environment rather than producing generic notification cards
- one-tap access to the relevant report
- no ordinary hamburger menu

The control entry is a compact holographic control node. It opens a radial/spatial control surface for Profile, Design Reality, Permissions, Data, Yansi and Settings.

## 7. Adaptive Intelligence Presentation

LIFEOZ should communicate intelligence through state, not decoration.

Example states:
- stable: quiet, low-energy environment
- opportunity: focused expansion around the relevant life domain
- conflict: connected paths visibly converge on the conflict
- prediction: future trajectory appears as a controlled extension of the current state
- action proposal: a clear reversible action appears with explanation
- confirmation required: sensitive action is explicitly requested from the user

## 8. Yansi Intelligence Contract

Yansi follows this conceptual loop:

**Observe → Understand → Connect → Predict → Explain → Ask → Act → Learn**

Where:
- Observe = read only permitted LifeOS/web/device context
- Understand = classify and interpret
- Connect = correlate multiple life domains
- Predict = identify likely future states with calibrated confidence
- Explain = show why an insight exists
- Ask = obtain confirmation for sensitive or consequential actions
- Act = execute only permitted/reversible actions
- Learn = retain user-approved history without self-rewriting application code

Voice path:

**User speech → speech recognition → Yansi reasoning → structured action/insight → user-visible confirmation where needed → TTS response**

## 9. First-Launch Experience

First launch is mandatory and must not be bypassed by accidentally opening the old dashboard.

Sequence:
1. LIFEOZ identity / welcome
2. User name/profile
3. Country/location
4. Currency
5. Language
6. Permission centre
7. Choose one of the six visual realities
8. Yansi introduction
9. Ready state

The chosen reality and profile persist across restarts.

## 10. Privacy / Memory

There must be no casual “Clear learning” action on the primary interface.

Memory controls belong in a dedicated data/privacy control surface. The user must be able to understand what is retained and what permissions are active. Destructive controls must require deliberate confirmation.

## 11. Web / External Intelligence

When the user permits it, Yansi may use approved web/search services for current information, research, comparisons and learning. Web use must be transparent and permission-controlled. Yansi must distinguish stored LifeOS information from externally retrieved information.

## 12. Interaction Rules

- No generic hamburger menu.
- No generic Material icons for final core glyphs.
- No permanent core labels on the home environment.
- No chatbot-first conversation screen as the primary experience.
- No fake hologram claims: effects must communicate actual state.
- No destructive memory reset on the main interface.
- No autonomous application self-rewrite.
- Sensitive actions require confirmation.
- Every visual state must have a functional meaning.

## 13. IP / Protection Workstream

This specification is intended to preserve a dated design record and a single source of truth. It does not itself create a patent or trademark registration.

Protection workstream:
1. Search the LIFEOZ word mark and relevant classes before filing.
2. Search similar logos/device marks before finalising the master logo.
3. Preserve dated source files and design iterations.
4. Identify copyrightable original artwork, glyphs, animations and UI assets.
5. Assess qualifying visual designs for design registration.
6. Identify genuinely technical inventions in Yansi/LIFEOZ architecture for patent counsel review before public disclosure.
7. Have an Indian trademark/patent professional conduct clearance and filing strategy.

For India, official IP India materials state that trademarks can protect distinctive words, logos and symbols, while patent examination for computer-related inventions is governed by the applicable CRI framework. Legal eligibility must be determined by the relevant professional and authority.

## 14. Implementation Gate

No future APK should be called “final hyper-futuristic” merely because it compiles.

A build can be called **LIFEOZ Brand/IP Design Conformant** only when:
- the locked logo system is implemented
- one of the six realities is visibly implemented end-to-end
- all five custom glyphs are used
- Yansi state animation is functional
- first-launch profile/location/currency/language flow works
- design selection works and persists
- holographic control node works
- voice → reasoning → response works
- memory/privacy controls follow this specification
- no old prototype dashboard leaks into the experience

## 15. Change Control

This file is the design baseline. Any change to the logo, core semantics, six realities, Yansi identity, interaction model or protection strategy must update this document before the implementation is changed.

**Target:** LIFEOZ must be recognisable as its own product language, not as a reskinned existing app.
