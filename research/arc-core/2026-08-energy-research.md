# iAmYansi Arc Core — Energy Research Baseline

## Goal
Investigate a physically real energy-generating core concept rather than a fictional perpetual-energy reactor. The system must convert an identifiable energy source into electricity and may use storage/buffering for output stability.

## Key finding — Russia
A 2025 Applied Physics Letters paper from the Ioffe Institute (St. Petersburg, Russia) and Crystallyne describes a photovoltaic nuclear battery using a self-glowing single-crystal IIb diamond activated with carbon-14 and an InGaP photoconverter. This establishes that the broad idea of a long-lived radioactive-diamond energy generator is already demonstrated/researched and is not a clean novelty claim for us.

A separate Russian patent, RU2461915C1, describes a nuclear battery using isotope material and layered semiconductor detectors to convert beta-particle energy into electrical current. Earlier Russian research also reported a diamond betavoltaic prototype supported by Russian facilities.

## What we should NOT claim
- We should not claim to be the first nuclear/diamond battery.
- We should not claim free energy or energy from nothing.
- We should not attempt to manufacture, concentrate, handle, or encapsulate radioactive isotopes in an uncontrolled workshop.
- We should not use a nuclear source in a house prototype without licensed nuclear/radiological engineering, regulatory approval, containment, shielding, monitoring, and professional testing.

## Potential differentiation
The research opportunity is an integrated Arc Core architecture combining:
1. A professionally sourced/regulated energy-generation module (future research option).
2. High-efficiency power conversion.
3. A safe electrical storage/buffer layer.
4. Independent hardware safety controller.
5. Thermal monitoring and management.
6. Yansi energy optimization, diagnostics and predictive maintenance.
7. Modular geometry so the same architecture can scale across device sizes.
8. A distinctive mechanical/thermal/electrical architecture that must undergo a formal prior-art and patentability review before any IP claim.

## Safe development path
Stage 1: non-radioactive electrical/thermal/solar test platform.
Stage 2: characterize conversion, power density, thermal behaviour and storage.
Stage 3: simulate candidate advanced-generation architectures.
Stage 4: if a nuclear/radioisotope pathway remains justified, work only with licensed specialist laboratories and regulatory authorities.

## Core engineering principle
Energy source -> energy conversion -> power conditioning -> buffer/storage -> protected output.

The Arc Core visual is an interface/enclosure concept; the actual energy source and power electronics remain physically and legally separated into controlled subsystems.
