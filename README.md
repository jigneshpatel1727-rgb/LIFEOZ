# AllInMyDay

AllInMyDay is the personal life-intelligence app. **iamyansi** is the ambient AI agent built inside AllInMyDay—not a separate app or a sixth core.

## MASTER VISUAL REFERENCE — LOCKED

The user-supplied design board remains the visual source of truth for the app and must be treated as a master layout, not optional inspiration.

### Home screen
- Central iamyansi living intelligence with luminous blue/gold energy form.
- Five unique intelligence symbols arranged around iamyansi in the finalized composition.
- No permanent core names on the home screen.
- Energy paths visibly connect all five symbols to iamyansi.
- Deep cosmic/neural background, particles, rings, glow and dimensional depth.
- Minimal interface: one screen, one tap, one report.

### Product architecture
- AllInMyDay is the application.
- iamyansi is the embedded AI intelligence/agent.
- The five cores are iamyansi's structured capabilities, not separate AI agents.
- Voice, memory, reasoning, permissions and verification belong to the iamyansi intelligence layer.
- iamyansi must remain an ambient/ghost presence unless the user activates it.

### Included visual systems
- AllInMyDay app mark and splash direction.
- Holographic Control: Profile, Design, Permissions, iamyansi and Settings.
- Adaptive Environment: Morning, Work, Evening, Focus and Rest.
- Six Visual Realities: Oreon Prime, Terra Flux, Vortex Nexus, Crysta Lumen, Nebula Soul and Shadow Core.

### Product rule
Design must not be downgraded to a conventional dashboard merely to simplify implementation. iamyansi is an ambient intelligence layer and must remain integrated with the master composition.
