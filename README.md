# LifeOZ

LifeOZ is a personal Life Operating System for day-to-day activity, expenses, calendar intelligence and Yansi AI.

## MASTER VISUAL REFERENCE — LOCKED

The user-supplied LifeOZ design board is the visual source of truth for the app. It must be treated as a **master layout**, not as optional inspiration.

### Home screen
- Central Yansi living intelligence with luminous blue/gold energy form.
- Five unique intelligence symbols arranged around Yansi in the same composition: top gold, upper-left green, upper-right violet, lower-left blue/time, lower-right violet/crystal.
- No permanent core names on the home screen.
- Energy paths visibly connect all five symbols to Yansi.
- Deep cosmic/neural background, particles, rings, glow and dimensional depth.
- Minimal interface: one screen, one tap, one report.

### Included visual systems
- LIFEOZ app mark and splash direction.
- Holographic Control: Profile, Design, Permissions, Yansi and Settings.
- Adaptive Environment: Morning, Work, Evening, Focus and Rest.
- Six Visual Realities: Oreon Prime, Terra Flux, Vortex Nexus, Crysta Lumen, Nebula Soul and Shadow Core.

### Product rule
Design is not to be downgraded to a conventional dashboard merely to simplify implementation. Yansi AI is an ambient intelligence layer and must be integrated into the visual system while preserving the master composition.
